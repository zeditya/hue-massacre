TINY SWORDS (Free Pack) - Pixel Frog
====================================

Source: https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords
File downloaded: "Tiny Swords (Free Pack).zip" (the free tier; the paid Enemy
Pack was NOT downloaded and nothing from it ships in this project).
Terms below copied from the pack's itch.io page on 2026-10-18. The zip itself
contains no licence file, so this file is the record of the page's terms at the
time of use - re-check the page if the project is ever published commercially.

LICENCE (verbatim from the itch.io page)
----------------------------------------

  Feel free to use this asset pack in both personal and commercial projects,
  modifying the assets as needed. Crediting is not required, but it helps and
  is always welcome. You may not redistribute, resell, or repackage the assets,
  even if the files are modified.

WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THIS PROJECT
--------------------------------

- Commercial use: allowed.
- Modification: allowed. Every sheet in assets/ is derived: resized with
  nearest-neighbour filtering and converted to greyscale so the game's
  hue_tint shader can recolour it. See tools/bake_itch_packs.sh.
- Redistribution: the RAW assets are never redistributed. Only the baked,
  recolour-ready derivatives ship, and only as part of the game - which is what
  the licence permits. The original zips are NOT in the repository.
- Credit: not required, but this project credits Pixel Frog anyway
  (see assets/ATTRIBUTION.md).

ALSO WORTH RECORDING
--------------------

- Pack grid: 64x64 px tiles. Animation speed: 10 fps (100 ms per frame).
- The author states on the page: "Content: No generative AI was used."
