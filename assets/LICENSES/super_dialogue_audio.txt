WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THIS PROJECT
--------------------------------
- MAY ship inside the game, commercially, and MAY be edited (trimmed, pitched,
  layered) - CC BY 4.0 is explicit about adaptation.
- **Attribution IS required**, and because we adapt (trim/pitch), the
  attribution must say so: "Dialogue voices - Super Dialogue Audio Pack v1 by
  Dillon Becker (dillonbecker.com), CC BY 4.0, edited (trimmed/pitched) for
  this game." It goes in `assets/ATTRIBUTION.md`, the README and the in-game
  credits, with the licence link.
- The raw pack is never committed (the .zip is gitignored); only the selected,
  converted .ogg files ship in `assets/audio/voice/`, each named after the line
  it serves so the mapping stays auditable.

WHAT IS IN THE PACK (1090 .wav, by category and voice actor)
------------------------------------------------------------
`Step 2 - Audio Files/` holds ten categories, each split `Male/` and `Female/`,
each of those split by actor (e.g. Alex Brodie, Ian Lampert, ...):

  1 Completion · 2 Confirmation · 3 Greeting · 4 Farewell · 5 Refusal ·
  6 Miscellaneous · 7 Damage · 8 Death · 9 Grunting · 10 Shouting

These are non-verbal performances (a breath, a grunt, a shout), which is exactly
what a Souls-like needs: the halls' sworn do not speak English, the *player*
reads. Planned use:
  - `7 Damage` / `8 Death` - the sworn react to being cut and to dying (one
    voice cast per hall, so a hall sounds like a place).
  - `9 Grunting` - the two-swing and heavy-swing exertions.
  - `10 Shouting` - the alarm shout that wakes the hall (the "oath" bark),
    and the boss's charge roar.
  - `3 Greeting` / `5 Refusal` - the Warden's line through the opening's gate.
  - `2 Confirmation` / `1 Completion` - the quarters-taken beats.
The English *words* still come from the barks on screen; the voices carry the
weight under them.
