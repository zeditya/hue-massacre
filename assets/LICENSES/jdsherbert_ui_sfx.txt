WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THIS PROJECT
--------------------------------
- MAY ship inside the game, commercially, modified (pitch/trim) as needed.
- **Credit IS required**: "JDSherbert" is credited in `assets/ATTRIBUTION.md`,
  the README and the in-game credits. The page's own terms page is
  https://jdsherbert.itch.io/terms-and-conditions.
- MUST NOT be redistributed as files: raw .wav/.mp3/.m4a files are never
  committed; only the converted .ogg files the game actually uses are staged in
  `assets/audio/ui/`.

WHAT IS WORTH TAKING
--------------------
- `Cursor` (30 variations) - the title card and the opening's "SPACE to
  continue" prompts.
- `Select` (12) / `Cancel` (12) - confirming a hall entry, backing out.
- `Popup Open` / `Popup Close` (6 each) - the hall banner, the hint line, the
  win/death pages.
- `Error` (5) - a guard breaking, a wall, an illegal action.
- `Swipe` (12) - the wheel strip and the verse card drawing on.
