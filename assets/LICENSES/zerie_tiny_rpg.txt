WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THIS PROJECT
--------------------------------

- Commercial use: allowed.
- Modification: allowed. assets/props/statue_soldier.png and statue_orc.png are
  frame 0 of the idle sheets, trimmed, converted to greyscale and scaled 2x so
  they stand at knight height (see tools/bake_itch_packs.sh).
- Redistribution: the raw sprites never leave the project; only the two baked
  statue sheets ship, as part of the game.
- AI training / NFT: not used, and never will be. The art is shipped as game
  content only.
- Credit: appreciated, not required. Given anyway in assets/ATTRIBUTION.md.
